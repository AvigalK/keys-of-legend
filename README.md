# Keys of Legend 🎹

Gamified piano lessons for a 61-key keyboard. The app listens through the phone's microphone and checks the notes you play and your timing.

**Open it:** https://avigalk.github.io/keys-of-legend/ (in Safari on iPhone, then Share → Add to Home Screen)

- **Learn**: World map with quests and boss battles (Worlds 0–6), sheet music, XP, levels, streaks
- **Song**: play a piece with a count-in; every note graded right/wrong and early/late
- **Hunt**: note-reading game on the grand staff
- **Tuner**: check the mic hears your keyboard

Single self-contained `index.html`, no build step. Progress is stored on the device (Settings → backup code).
