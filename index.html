Custom piece format
E4/3 E4q/3 F4h/4 G4q./5 Rq E4+C3/3
note + octave (C4 = middle C), duration w h q e s (default q), . = dotted, /3 = finger, Rq = rest, + joins notes played together (hands together), | bar lines ignored.
